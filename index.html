Frequently Asked Questions.

Enrollment How does enrollment work and how quickly do I get started? +

Enrollment is instant. When you create your free account, our automated system generates temporary login credentials and delivers them immediately to your email inbox. You can log into your candidate dashboard right away, choose from available courses such as Android Enterprise Foundations, Workspace ONE Essentials, SOTI MobiControl, or Ivanti Neurons, and begin learning at your own pace.

Lab Access How does hands-on lab access work and what are the prerequisites? +

Each module includes cloud-hosted interactive lab scenarios mimicking real enterprise production environments. All labs are accessible directly through modern web browsers without requiring complex local virtual machines or high-end workstations. Tutor instructors upload guided lab manuals, policy walk-throughs, and sample configurations that you can download directly from your dashboard.

Lab Access Can I practice on live endpoint management consoles? +

Yes! Our coursework walks you through configuring Managed Google Play, Zero-touch enrollment profiles, compliance validation policies, and conditional access rules in live sandbox tenants. Instructors provide real-world exercises with immediate feedback.

Certification Are certificates provided upon course completion? +

Yes. After completing all modular video walkthroughs, passing the end-of-module knowledge assessments, and submitting lab exercises, you will receive a verifiable UEM Labs Academy Certified Specialist digital credential. You can share your badge on LinkedIn, embed it into resumes, or download a high-resolution PDF certificate.

Enrollment Can teams or enterprise IT departments enroll together? +

Absolutely. We offer dedicated tutor dashboards for enterprise team leads and corporate trainers. Tutors can assign tailored cohorts, distribute custom course materials and proprietary policy blueprints, and monitor completion metrics across their entire team.
